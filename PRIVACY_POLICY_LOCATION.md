# Privacy Policy - Rose HR App
## Location Data Usage Disclosure

**Last Updated:** April 12, 2026

---

## 1. Location Data Collection

### What We Collect
Rose HR ("we", "our", "the app") collects location data from your device when you use our attendance tracking features.

**Types of Location Data:**
- **Precise Location (GPS)**: Exact geographical coordinates
- **Background Location**: Location data even when the app is in the background or closed
- **Approximate Location**: Network-based location (cell towers, Wi-Fi)

### When We Collect Location Data
Location data is collected in the following situations:
- When you clock in for work (check-in)
- When you clock out from work (check-out)
- During attendance verification
- When tracking work hours at designated locations

---

## 2. Purpose of Location Data Collection

We collect location data for the following business purposes:

### Primary Purposes:
1. **Attendance Verification**: Verify that employees are physically present at authorized work locations when clocking in/out
2. **Work Location Tracking**: Ensure attendance is recorded only at approved company premises
3. **Time & Attendance Management**: Accurately track employee work hours and locations
4. **Payroll Processing**: Generate accurate payroll based on verified location-based attendance
5. **Compliance & Auditing**: Maintain records for labor law compliance and internal audits

### Legal Basis:
- Legitimate business interest in verifying employee attendance
- Contractual necessity (employment agreement)
- Compliance with labor laws and regulations

---

## 3. How We Use Location Data

### Data Usage:
- **Attendance Records**: Create timestamped attendance records with location verification
- **Reporting**: Generate attendance reports for managers and HR administrators
- **Geofencing**: Verify employee is within authorized work area boundaries
- **Analytics**: Aggregate attendance patterns (no individual tracking outside work hours)

### What We DO NOT Do:
- ❌ Track your location outside of work hours (unless you're clocked in)
- ❌ Monitor your location for personal activities
- ❌ Sell your location data to third parties
- ❌ Use location data for advertising or marketing
- ❌ Share location data with unauthorized parties

## 7. Your Rights & Controls

### You Have the Right To:
1. **Access**: Request a copy of your location data
2. **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate data
3. **Deletion**: Request deletion of your data (subject to legal requirements)
4. **Opt-Out**: Disable location permissions (may limit app functionality)
5. **Portability**: Receive your data in a common format

### How to Exercise Your Rights:
- **In-App**: Manage location permissions in device settings
- **Contact HR**: Email [hr@roseholding.com] or contact your HR department
- **Data Request**: Submit formal data request via [contact method]

### Disabling Location Access:
**Android:**
1. Go to Settings > Apps > Rose HR > Permissions
2. Tap "Location" and select "Don't allow"

**iOS:**
1. Go to Settings > Privacy > Location Services > Rose HR
2. Select "Never" or "While Using the App"

**Note**: Disabling location will prevent attendance check-in/out functionality.

## 10. Background Location Usage (Android)

### Why We Need Background Location:
Rose HR requests background location permission to:
- Continue tracking work hours if you switch to another app while clocked in
- Verify location during scheduled shifts
- Enable accurate attendance records for shift workers

### How We Limit Background Usage:
- Only active when you are clocked in
- Automatically disabled when you clock out
- Location checks are periodic, not continuous
- Battery-optimized to minimize power consumption
